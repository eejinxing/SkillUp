# Program Leader Feature Walkthrough (Demo Script)

- Role: Program Leader
- Demo account: `sarah` / `Demo1234!`
- Length: about 6–8 minutes
- Format: **[Action]** = what you click or do on screen; "Quote" = what you say

---

## 0. Opening (about 30 seconds)

"Hello everyone. Today I'll walk you through the **Program Leader** role in Skill UP.
Skill UP has four roles. Learners study, Instructors create courses, Admins manage accounts, and the Program Leader sits in the middle: they **manage learning programs, safeguard course quality, and keep track of learning results**.
The navigation has five entries: **Overview, Programs, Instructors, Course approvals and Messages**. A Program Leader can also open Analytics and Learners. Let's go through them one by one."

**[Action]** Log in page → enter `sarah` → sign in. You land on the Dashboard.

---

## 1. Overview Dashboard (about 1.5 minutes)

**[Action]** Stay on the Dashboard and point out each area from top to bottom.

"The first thing you see after signing in is the **Overview dashboard**. At a glance it shows everything in the programs I lead."

1. **Metric cards:** "Key numbers such as managed courses, learners and pending reviews."
2. **Weekly Platform Activity chart:** "This chart shows platform activity and compares it with the **prior period**. The buttons above it change the time range."
   **[Action]** Switch the period buttons and let the chart update.
3. **Course performance:** "Pick a course from the dropdown to see its details, its completion rate and a **learner breakdown**."
   **[Action]** Change the course, then open the "more" menu to show three shortcuts: **View course, Edit course, Program analytics**.
4. **Learning summary:** "A short summary of overall learning health."
5. **Alerts & notifications:** "The notification centre on the right can be filtered into **Unread, Read and Archived**. Open a notification to act on it directly. At the bottom, a link shows how many reviews are pending and opens the approvals page."
   **[Action]** Open the filter and switch Unread → Read.
6. **Quick navigation rail on the left:** "Every feature is one click away from this icon column."

---

## 2. Programs (about 2 minutes)

**[Action]** Click **Programs** in the sidebar.

"A **program groups several courses into a learning path** that learners follow in order. This is where I manage them."

### 2.1 Program list
- "Each program is a card, and the **search box** at the top finds programs by name."
  **[Action]** Type a keyword in the search box.
- "I only see **programs assigned to me**. Other leaders' programs are hidden, which is the access control."

### 2.2 Create a program
**[Action]** Click **+ New program**.
- "Enter the **program name and description**, then use the switch to decide whether to **publish the learning path**. Only published programs appear in learners' learning paths."
- **[Action]** Click **Save program**. You can also submit it empty to show the required-field validation.

### 2.3 Edit or delete a program
- "The card menu offers **Manage courses, Edit details and Delete program**."
- "Note that **only a program with no courses can be deleted**, which prevents accidents."

### 2.4 Manage the learning path (key feature)
**[Action]** Open a program.
- Left summary: "Program status (Published or Draft), number of assigned courses, courses in the saved path, and enrolments."
- **Learning path section:** "This sets the **order learners see**. Use the **up and down arrows** to reorder and **Remove** to take a course out of the path. The course stays assigned to the program."
  **[Action]** Move one course down, click **Save path** to apply it, then show **Discard path changes**.
- **Other courses in this program:** "Courses not yet in the path. **Add to path** puts them in."
- **All assigned courses:** "The full list of courses under this program."
- **Move program:** "A course can be **moved to another program I manage**. Materials, enrolments and learner progress are kept, but a course awaiting review must be withdrawn first."
- **Create a course in this program / Add existing course:** "I can also create a new course here or bring in an existing one."

---

## 3. Instructors (about 45 seconds)

**[Action]** Click **Instructors** in the sidebar (the Assignments page).

"Here I decide **which instructor is responsible for each course**."
- **[Action]** Search for a course or an instructor.
- **[Action]** Choose a course → **Assign instructor** → pick an instructor → **Save assignment**.
- "This keeps every course connected to the right instructor, who can then edit its content."

---

## 4. Course approvals (about 2 minutes, key feature)

**[Action]** Click **Course approvals** in the sidebar.

"When an instructor submits a course, it does not go live automatically. A Program Leader reviews it first. This is the core of **quality control**."

### 4.1 The Requests board
- "All **create/update** and **delete** applications for my programs are collected here. Switch between the **Board** and **Table** views."
  **[Action]** Toggle Board ↔ Table.
- **Date strip:** "See what was submitted on a given day. The arrows move 12 days back or forward, and **All dates** clears the date filter."
- **Filters:** "Search by course, instructor or request, and filter by **application type** (Create/update or Delete) and by **program**. The number of matching applications is shown above the results."

### 4.2 Review one application
**[Action]** Open an application.
- "The page shows the **request, the course as it stands, and a progress timeline**."
- **[Action]** Click **Start review**.
- **Your decision** panel on the right:
  - For a course: **Approve (publish it), Reject, or Request changes**.
  - For a deletion: **approve removes the course, reject keeps it**.
  - "**Feedback is required** and goes straight to the instructor."
  **[Action]** Choose Request changes → write feedback → **Submit decision**.
- "After submission the application becomes read-only and the decision is added to the timeline, so there is a full record."
- "If an application does not belong to one of my programs, the system shows **Request access denied**. I cannot act outside my own programs."

---

## 5. Program analytics (about 1 minute)

**[Action]** From the Dashboard open **Program analytics** (the Analytics page).

"This page is for **data**."
- Top stat cards: overall figures.
- **Programs table:** courses, enrolled, completion and quiz average for each program.
- **Courses table:** enrolled, **participation**, completion and quiz average for each course.
- **[Action]** Use **All / Active / No enrolments** to find courses nobody has joined.
- **[Action]** Click **Load More** for further rows.
- **[Action]** Click **Export CSV** to download the report, or **Print**.
- "The numbers come from real enrolments and submitted quiz attempts. Participation counts enrolments with at least one completed activity."

---

## 6. Learners (about 30 seconds)

**[Action]** Click **Learners** at the top right of Analytics.

"This is the **learner table**: learner, course, progress, status and last active time."
- **[Action]** Click **View** to open one learner's full record.
- "If needed I can **Remove** an enrolment. The system asks for confirmation first."

---

## 7. Messages (about 20 seconds)

**[Action]** Click **Messages**.

"A Program Leader can **message instructors and learners** directly and use course channels and announcements. For example, during a review I can message the instructor to discuss the feedback."

---

## 8. Closing (about 20 seconds)

"To sum up, the Program Leader has five main capabilities:

1. **Overview:** see program health and pending alerts at a glance
2. **Programs:** create programs and arrange the learning path
3. **Instructors:** assign an instructor to every course
4. **Course approvals:** review creation and deletion requests and give feedback
5. **Analytics and Learners:** track results and learner progress with data

This means every course that goes live has an owner, a reviewer and numbers to check. Thank you."
